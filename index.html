Research

My research focuses on trustworthy AI systems, including self-evolving AI agents, efficient agentic RAG, activation steering, and post-training for long-horizon code agents. I aim to develop continuous-control harnesses that coordinate self-evolution across multiple timescales, from fast agent-level adaptation to slower model-level updates, enabling continual learning from long-horizon interaction and feedback. Any form of constructive feedback and collaboration is welcome; feel free to send me an email.
